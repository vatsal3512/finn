# Research scripts

One-off scripts from 27 October 2026, used to choose the portfolio and research companies before the data layer existed. They are kept for the build history, not as part of the pipeline.

- `nse_test.py` checks that NSE's announcements API answers from a home connection.
- `scan.py` pulled 30 days of NSE filings for 57 candidate companies.
- `px.py` measured six months of volatility and volume spikes from Yahoo Finance.

The raw outputs are git-ignored. The reasoning they fed is in PROGRESS_LOG.md, decisions D1 to D3.
